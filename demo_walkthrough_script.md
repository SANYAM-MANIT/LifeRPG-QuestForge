# 🎬 Video Walkthrough Checklist (90–180s)

A quick overview of the key features demonstrated in the project video:

1. **User Authentication**: Sign up or log in to a secure hero profile with isolated database records.
2. **Quest Management (CRUD)**: Create a categorized quest (e.g. *DSA Study*, Hard difficulty, Intellect stat).
3. **Task Completion & Feedback**: Complete a quest to hear the 8-bit chime, see floating combat numbers (`+XP`, `+Gold`), and damage the Dungeon Boss.
4. **Non-Linear Leveling**: Trigger the Level-Up celebration modal with canvas confetti and stat boosts.
5. **Shop & Inventory**: Purchase gear or health potions in the Bazaar and equip them to boost hero stats.
6. **Database Persistence**: Refresh the browser (`F5` / `Cmd + R`) to prove all character progress is securely saved in the backend SQLite database.
