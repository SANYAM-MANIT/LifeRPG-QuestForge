## 💡 Pro-Tips for Recording
1. **Screen Recorder**: Use OBS Studio, Loom, or Mac QuickTime Player (`Cmd + Shift + 5`).
2. **Audio**: Turn on your computer audio so the 8-bit sound effects (quest chime, level-up fanfare) can be heard clearly.
3. **File Size**: Export at 1080p/720p (MP4 format). Ensure file size is **strictly under 100MB** as required by the rubric.
